Every bank, market infrastructure, and fintech is building its own chain, issuing its own tokens, and connecting to everyone else through a patchwork of bilateral bridges. Left unresolved, that could leave tokenised finance as a collection of disconnected chains that can’t talk to each other. That path leads somewhere we’ve seen before.
