A joint paper from Quant and Murex on how tokenisation can scale within institutions’ existing trading, risk and post-trade workflows. Tokenisation is expanding across capital markets, but scaling it remains hard. Institutions are working across fragmented settlement infrastructures, integrating digital assets into existing operating models, and coordinating cash that often still moves on conventional rails.
